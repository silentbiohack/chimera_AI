# CHIMERA — Investor Pitch

## The thesis in one line
**Every enterprise will deploy AI agents in 2026. None of them have an immune system. CHIMERA *is* that immune system.**

## The problem
- Enterprise AI agents have **excessive agency** (tools, memory, multi-step plans)
- The attack surface is **non-deterministic** — every prompt is a new threat
- Static red-teaming reports go stale in **weeks**
- Traditional SOCs cannot reason about AI-native threats (prompt injection,
  memory poisoning, RAG corruption, agent-swarm trust abuse)
- Regulators (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001) require *continuous*
  adversarial validation — there is no incumbent product

## The product
CHIMERA is an autonomous AI-vs-AI security platform. AI attacker swarms
continuously discover, mutate, and evolve exploits against the customer's AI
agent estate. A parallel AI defender layer — anchored on the Veea Lobster
Trap policy fabric — classifies attacks, synthesizes new policies, and
A/B-tests them in the arena before promoting.

Three pillars:
1. **Attack Arena** — AI-vs-AI battles, streamed live
2. **Attack Genome** — exploit DNA, mutation lineage, family clustering
3. **Defense Core** — versioned, auto-generated policies with full audit

## The wedge
Most "AI security" today is a static prompt-injection benchmark or a content
filter. CHIMERA's wedge is **continuous adversarial evolution** — the same
shape as a SOC, but operating at AI tempo and AI semantics.
